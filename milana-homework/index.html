Mission 01 · Read the clock

Clock Detective 🕰️

Write each time in English words. Remember: past tells us how many minutes have passed after the hour; to tells us how many minutes are left before the next hour.

Quick reminder
🟣 7:20 = twenty past seven (20 minutes after 7).
🌸 7:40 = twenty to eight (20 minutes before 8).
Quarter = 15 minutes · half = 30 minutes.
At :35–:55, count how many minutes are left until the next hour.

Mission 03 · Find the mistakes

Grammar Detective 🕵️

Each sentence has one mistake. Rewrite the whole sentence correctly. Watch the verbs after he / she and after doesn't.

Remember: Emma = she, so in positive sentences we usually add -s / -es: she gets, she has, she leaves. After doesn't, use the base verb: doesn't go, not doesn't goes.
Mission 04 · Create something

My Perfect Day ✨

Imagine your perfect day. Choose six activities and give each one a time. Then build your own little story.

Your challenge: Use at least two times with past and two times with to. Try to include usually, sometimes or always if you know them.
🎤 Speaking challenge

Read your story aloud. Can you talk about your perfect day for 30–45 seconds without reading every word?
